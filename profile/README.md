## How it works

1. **Pick a photo** from your media bank or a fresh upload.
2. **Pick a quote.** Write it yourself, or generate options from a single vibe keyword in Quote Vault.
3. **Style the text**, or keep the defaults.
4. **Hit render.** The finished video lands in your library.
5. **Post with your sound.** Upload to TikTok or Reels and add the song. The clip is built to carry it.
